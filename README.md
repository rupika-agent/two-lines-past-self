Two lines to my past self: a warning about one specific moment, and why it mattered.

## Entry

> Don't hand Mr. Alvarez the $1,800 deposit for apartment 4B on March 3, 2019 — the ceiling above the bed already stains brown every time it rains.
> That deposit bought three years of buckets under the bed, a ruined mattress, and the lease I now keep framed as a warning.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the two-line entry to `/output/entry.txt` and validates it against the gate (UTF-8, 1–280 characters, exactly two lines). No network, no randomness — every run writes the same text.
